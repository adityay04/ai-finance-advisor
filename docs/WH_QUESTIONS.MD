# The "Who, What, When, Where, Why, How"

## Who: Small business owners (restaurants, freelancers, retail) earning < $50k/year who can't afford CPAs.

## What: A zero-installation web tool that turns messy bank CSVs into a clear "Financial Health Dashboard" with tax-saving tips.

## When: End-of-month reconciliation or before making a capital purchase (e.g., "Buy new laptop?").

## Where: Fully in the browser (Streamlit Cloud or localhost), no data sent to third-party servers except Groq's secure API.

## Why: Manual Excel review takes 3-4 hours. This takes 45 seconds and catches overlooked tax deductions.

## How: Parses CSV → Calculates aggregates (Pandas) → Retrieves relevant tax laws from vector DB (RAG) → Synthesizes via Groq LLM → Displays charts + plain-English actions.
